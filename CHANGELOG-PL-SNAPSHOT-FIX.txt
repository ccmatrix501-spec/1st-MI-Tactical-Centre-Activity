PL Snapshot / AAR fix
=====================

1. PL Snapshot now works while everyone is still together in Briefing Room 1/2/3.
   - Dropship 1 -> Briefing Room 1
   - Dropship 2 -> Briefing Room 2
   - Dropship 3 -> Briefing Room 3
   - The bot still checks the dedicated squad VCs first.
   - Briefing Room members are sorted by their server-name tag:
       [D] = Demon
       [N] = Nightmare
       [C] = Cerberus
       [H] = Hellfire
   - The person who clicks the PL button is stored as Platoon Lead and is not also counted as a squad member.
   - Members without one of those tags are kept in the operation roster and shown as Unassigned.

2. Squad Leads are still selected during the AAR, not at PL Snapshot time.

3. Snapshot AAR screen now has:
   - Continue AAR
   - Use Voice Channel Instead
   - Select Manually Instead

4. When no PL Snapshot is being used, the AAR shows the Voice Channel selector immediately.

5. Choosing Voice Channel or Manual explicitly clears the snapshot from that AAR so the report uses the selected roster instead.

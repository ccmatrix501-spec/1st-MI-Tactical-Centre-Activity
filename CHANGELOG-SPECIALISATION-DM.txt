Specialization Question DM Notifications
=========================================

- When a Specialization Question change is confirmed in the Tactical Centre editor,
  the bot now privately messages Discord user ID 295215176372846592.
- The DM includes:
  - who made the change
  - specialization/company
  - question number
  - old question text
  - new question text
  - timestamp
- If the DM cannot be delivered, the question change still saves and the error is
  written to the bot console.

AAR Squad Lead selection update

- PL Snapshot now saves immediately when the PL claims a dropship.
- Snapshot records the PL and the occupants of Platoon Lead, Demon, Nightmare, Cerberus and Hellfire VCs.
- Squad Leads are no longer selected at PL Snapshot time.
- During the AAR, the latest PL Snapshot roster auto-loads.
- The AAR then asks the PL to select Squad Leads from the members snapshotted in each squad.
- Demon, Nightmare and Hellfire leads are required when occupied; Cerberus remains optional.
- Selected Squad Leads are attached to that AAR only and included in the AAR roster/report data.
